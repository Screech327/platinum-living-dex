# Platinum Living Dex

A tracker for a Pokémon Platinum living dex: all 493 Pokémon, one of each, boxed in a single save. It runs as a static site on GitHub Pages: **https://screech327.github.io/platinum-living-dex/**

## What's in it

- **Sinnoh**: Platinum's 210-Pokémon regional dex for your story run. Mark each one seen and caught. Every entry says where to catch it during the story and which trainers use it (from the game's trainer data), and flags rare sightings. See all 210 to unlock the National Dex.
- **Living**: search any Pokémon by name or number and tap to check it off. Filter by method, by when it unlocks (story, after Hall of Fame, after National Dex, other game), by source cart, or by region.
- **Detail panel**: tap a card for its sprite, source game, how it gets into Platinum, step-by-step requirements (including what you have to finish in other games), encounter locations with rates and levels, the evolution family, how many copies to catch, and your own notes.
- **Boxes**: your 18 PC boxes in National Dex order. Slots fill in as you box Pokémon.
- **Plan**: progress grouped by game, by unlock stage, or by region.
- **Today**: real-world timers for honey trees, swarms, Trophy Garden, Great Marsh, Feebas tiles, Pal Park and Drifloon Fridays.
- **Copies**: every evolution line that needs duplicates, with a counter.
- **More**: badges, a progress code for moving progress between devices, backup/restore, and reset.

## Your progress is safe across updates

Progress is stored in your browser (`localStorage`, key `pld-v1`), not in these files, so pushing a new version of the site never resets it. Each browser and device keeps its own copy; use the progress code or a backup file to move it between devices.

## Data

- `data.js` is generated by `tools/site_data.py` from the [PokeAPI](https://github.com/PokeAPI/pokeapi) encounter and evolution tables.
- Trainer teams and map positions come from [pret/pokeplatinum](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum).
- Special cases (legendaries, other-cart sources, events, fossils, gifts) are hand-written and were checked against Bulbapedia and Serebii.
- Sprites load at view time from the PokeAPI sprite repository.
